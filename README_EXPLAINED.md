# 🌌 Aether OS: Your Intelligent Agency Workspace

Welcome to **Aether OS**! If you are new to coding or business management tools, don't worry. This guide will explain everything in simple terms so you can start scaling your agency today.

---

## 🧐 What is Aether OS?
Think of Aether OS as a **"Smart Office"** that lives in your computer. It is designed for people who run "Agencies" (businesses that provide services like marketing, design, or coding to other companies).

It helps you:
1. **Track your money** (in Naira ₦).
2. **Organize your tasks** so you never miss a deadline.
3. **Talk to an AI Assistant** that knows your business and helps you make decisions.

---

## 🧩 Key Terms Explained

### 1. What is "Aether"?
In science fiction and ancient philosophy, *Aether* was the mysterious element that filled the universe and connected everything. 
In this project, **Aether** is the name of your **AI Assistant**. It is the "brain" of the operation that connects your clients, your tasks, and your money.

### 2. What are "Nodes"?
In computer science, a *Node* is just a point where information is stored or processed. 
In Aether OS, we use the word **Nodes** to describe the different parts of your business:
*   **Client Node**: Information about a company you work with.
*   **Financial Node**: A record of an invoice or payment.
*   **Task Node**: A specific job you need to finish.
*   **Neural Link**: This is just a fancy way of saying your computer is "connected" to the database or the AI.

## 🚀 How to Use Aether OS

### 🏡 The Dashboard
This is your **Home Base**. It gives you a "bird's eye view" of your business. If you see "0" everywhere, that's okay! It just means you are a new user. As you add clients and tasks, these numbers will grow automatically.

### 🤖 The AI Agent
Click on **AI Agent** in the sidebar to talk to Aether. You can ask it things like:
*   *"What should my marketing strategy be for a new client?"*
*   *"How much have I earned this month?"*
Aether remembers your past conversations, so you can always pick up where you left off.

### 📋 Kanban Board (Coming Soon!)
This is a digital board with columns like "To Do" and "Done." You can drag and drop your tasks to see exactly what needs your attention right now.

### 💰 Invoices (Coming Soon!)
Professional documents you can send to clients to get paid in Naira. When they pay, your Dashboard updates automatically!

## 🛠️ Technical Stuff (For Beginners)
*   **Supabase**: This is your "Digital Filing Cabinet." It stores all your chats and client info securely so they don't disappear when you refresh the page.
*   **Next.js**: The "Engine" that makes the website fast and smooth.
*   **Lucide Icons**: The little pictures (like the bell and settings gear) you see on the screen.
